AI/LLM Policy (2026•11•10)

interpunctual site contributors have participated in academic and organizational discourse around using ai tools during production. the following ai/llm policy is based on a template drafted for democratic socialists of america chapters by its national tech committee. though it is not formally affiliated with the democratic socialists of america, interpunctual site adopts the following ai/llm policy.

OVERVIEW

  1. acceptable use
    1. contributors are expected to disclose when they use ai and for what it was used for; contributors must be able to explain in detail what code they’ve submitted does.
    2. ai use and abstinence must both be intentional; what matters is the quality of the contribution.
  2. maintainer expectations
    1. regardless of how it was written: code, configs, or other ai generated output are expected to be assessed equally; namely passing necessary tests & security reviews.
    2. project maintainers are expected to make upfront disclosure of ai use part of their projects, either via pull request templates or some other way of encouraging members to openly state when they use it.
    3. no official interpunctual site work shall require the use of machine learning models or license to machine learning models that are proprietary or have been trained on proprietary data in order to contribute to that project.
  3. unacceptable use
    1. documentation (excluding: in-code comments and commit messages), correspondence (including but not limited to: tickets, pull requests, forum, email, or chats), or other governance documents must be written by contributors.
    2. using interpunctual site data for training any generative ai models is prohibited.
    3. any sound, artwork, graphics, or images used in interpunctual site projects must not be ai generated.
  4. provider selection
    1. ai algorithms and tools provided by twitter/x.com/xai are prohibited and contributions from these tools must be rejected.
    2. contributors should ideally look to the following criteria for inspiration on which providers to use:
      1. prohibition on any ai providers if they are bds targets
      2. investigate feasibility of “better” providers (if any exist) per our software rubric, namely:
        • unionized staff or run by a worker cooperative
        • open source/open weight (if possible)
        • self-hosted (where feasible)
        • provider has zero data retention for any models that will be used - no prompts, inputs, or any other data should be retained by the provider unless explicitly provided by the user
  5. policy updates
    1. this policy may be amended by contributors at will.
    2. contributors will be responsible for rectifying conflicting policy items and announcing changes.
