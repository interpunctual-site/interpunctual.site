Method

  1. pre-production: 10 minutes
    • veggie sauté and garbanzo beans
      • dice the onions and bell pepper.
      • drain and rinse garbanzo beans.
    • grains
      • rinse the quinoa and rice separately.
      • heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
      • heat dry skillet over medium-high heat.
      • combine melted butter with water in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil.
      • bring a small saucepan of water to a boil.
  2. production: 30 minutes
    • veggie sauté and garbanzo beans
      • place onions and bell pepper into a large skillet with olive oil.
      • add and stir in minced garlic.
      • sauté vegetables until their edges are slightly charred.
      • fold in garbanzo beans seasoned with salt and pepper.
    • grains
      • toast the quinoa in a dry skillet until lightly browned.
      • pour rice into a medium saucepan, then salt and stir.
      • salt and pepper the quinoa, then pour into a small saucepan.
      • cover medium saucepan and simmer for 15 minutes.
      • cover small saucepan and simmer for 7.5 minutes.
      • add buttered, salted, and peppered frozen corn to small saucepan.
      • cover small saucepan and simmer for 7.5 minutes.
    • quinoa fried rice
      • remove the medium saucepan from heat and leave covered for 10 minutes before fluffing.
      • mix quinoa, corn, and rice into the sautéed vegetables in a large skillet on medium-high heat for 2.5 minutes.
    • mango curry sauce
      • add coconut milk, curry paste, turmeric, cayenne, paprika, coriander, brown sugar, and mango into a small saucepan.
      • bring the sauce to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer until mango is tender (approximately 2.5 minutes).
  3. post-production: 5 minutes
    • quinoa fried rice: serve quinoa fried rice filling in a bowl.
    • mango curry sauce: pour mango curry sauce over the quinoa fried rice.
