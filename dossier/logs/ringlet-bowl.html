Method

  1. pre-production: 15 minutes
    • pour two cans of cannellini beans into a pot without draining aquafaba and simmer for about 10 minutes.
    • slice one red onion thinly and finely chop a bunch of fresh parsley before setting aside.
    • strain the beans and allow to cool before transferring to mixing bowl.
  2. production: 5 minutes
    • add sliced onion and chopped parsley to the mixing bowl.
    • add lemon juice and white vinegar to the mixing bowl.
    • season with salt, pepper, and sumac to taste.
    • add a drizzle of olive oil to the mixing bowl.
  3. post-production: 120 minutes
    • toss the ingredients in the mixing bowl to combine thoroughly before transferring to storage container.
    • allow the mixture to rest in the fridge for two hours to let the flavors meld before serving.
