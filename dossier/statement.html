statement

motivated by a desire to cultivate presence and build community, i create music, films, software, graphics, literature, and environments that hold space for change. this involves using emerging modes of communication to transform would-be consumers of culture into intentional participants in its production by prompting confrontations with uncertainty. these works grow out of lived experience as a working-class, black queer pagan immigrant and thrive in the industrial, psychedelic, and feminist afrofuturist milieus.

themes that recur throughout my catalogue include memory, repression, alienation, temporality, and sustainability. having graduated from the bass school at ut dallas, i approach these issues using a methodology that incorporates sociological inquiry and media arts. synthesising these insights with others gained from ten years as a freelance videographer, my interdisciplinary workflow traces the contours of filmmaking: pre-production, production, and post-production.

faced with post-industrial automation and apocalyptic spectacle within an increasingly exploitative attention economy, the only viable response is to embrace vulnerability and free association. both factor into alternative modes of developing and distributing value outside of the incentive structures legitimised by unaccountable authorities. liberation from the borders of psychological, social, and physical enclosures protects the commons, fertile ground wherein every reality germinates like a seed. to this end—as a matter of survival—i am devoted to maintaining a relationship with creative power through my practice.
